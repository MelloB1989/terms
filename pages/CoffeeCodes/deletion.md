🗑️ User Account & Data Deletion Policy

At NoteWise, we respect your right to privacy and give you full control over your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can do so easily.

🔁 How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your account and all related data through any of the following methods:

Option 1: From Within the App
	•	Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account
	•	Confirm your identity (if required)
	•	Tap Delete My Account

Your data will be permanently deleted within 7 working days.

Option 2: Via Email Request

Email us at contact@noobsverse.com using the subject line:
“Delete My NoteWise Account”
Please include your registered email or user ID for verification.

⸻

🧹 What Will Be Deleted

Upon deletion:
	•	Your personal information (name, email, etc.)
	•	All your uploaded notes, saved content, and study activity
	•	All data stored on our servers linked to your account

This process is irreversible. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

⸻

⏳ Timeline
	•	We process all verified deletion requests within 7 business days.
	•	You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been permanently removed.

⸻

🔐 Data Retention Exceptions

We may retain minimal data if:
	•	Required by law (e.g., financial, legal records)
	•	Necessary to resolve disputes or enforce our Terms of Service

⸻

For any questions, feel free to contact us at team@noobsverse.com
